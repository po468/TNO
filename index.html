Letters from the Road

Someone passing through left a small message here. If you found a tag, you are in the right place.

How to play

  1. Tap the tag with your phone. It should open a page like /d/wind-19.
  2. Read the riddle. The answer is the passcode.
  3. Enter the code to open that stop’s letter, photo, and stamp.

No app. No account. If nothing happens when you tap, your phone may need NFC turned on, or the tag may be sitting on metal.

What this is

A cross-country drive, written in pieces and stuck to the world. Each drop is its own short encounter — not a chain you have to finish.

Where they are

Planted along the route, usually at places people already linger: overlooks, rest stops, trailheads, town benches. This page will list regions, not exact hides.
